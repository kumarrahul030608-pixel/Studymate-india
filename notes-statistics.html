Mean

Arithmetic Mean = Σx / n

For frequency data: Mean = Σfx / Σf

Median

Arrange observations in ascending order. If n is odd, median is the middle value. If n is even, median is the average of the two middle values.

Mode

Mode is the value that occurs most frequently. For grouped data, use the modal-class formula.

Variance & Standard Deviation

Population variance = Σ(x − x̄)² / n

Standard deviation = √variance

Frequency Distribution

Frequency tells how many times a value or class occurs. Histograms, frequency polygons and ogives are common graphical presentations.
